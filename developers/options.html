      Options

      Options data in StrateScript

      Read options chains and option trades from your scripts, turn them into levels and flows on your futures chart, alert on them, and use them as context in simulated strategies. The same script runs live, in Replay and in long backtests.

      • SPX · NDX · SPY · QQQ
      • Live · Replay · long backtest
      • Strategies simulated on ES, MES, NQ, MNQ

      What options data lets you build#

      StrateScript reads two kinds of options data, for the indexes SPX and NDX and the ETFs SPY and QQQ:

      • Chain snapshots, with options.chain(): the whole chain of one underlying at one moment. For each option: strike, expiry, call or put, settlement, open interest, gamma and implied volatility. On top of that, Strate computes gamma exposure, dealer vanna and charm, the zero-gamma level, and the conversion of every strike into a price on your futures chart.
      • Option trades, with on option_trade, on option_block and on option_feed: every print one by one, with its side, greeks and amounts, plus the orders, bursts and multi-leg structures (verticals, straddles, condors, calendars…) that Strate builds from them.

      Use options data alone, or together with bars, trades and order-by-order (MBO) data in the same script.

      Options are read, never traded.

      Strategies trade only the futures (ES, MES, NQ, MNQ), and only in simulation. Your options data connection is read only: Strate never sends an order through it.

      Chart families. On ES and MES the chart's options family is SPX (and its ETF, SPY). On NQ and MNQ it is NDX (and QQQ). options.chain() with no argument reads the family's index, so the same script works on ES and on NQ. Strikes of the chart's family are converted into prices on your futures chart (another family needs basis: or ratio:).

      Where the data comes from#

      Your own options data connection

      Each user connects their own options data account, in Strate's connections. It gives real-time chain snapshots: Strate assembles one every 10 seconds by default (10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds, one setting for charts and scripts). Option trades arrive print by print.

      The Connections window shows the options data account Strate supports, and the app's options displays tell you when it is missing or needs checking.

      A delayed public source

      When your connection gives nothing usable (no connection, a connection that is down, still loading or stale), a public source delayed by 15 minutes takes over for the chain. It is the snapshot the app already shows on its charts; scripts receive it only while the app shows it. A script can tell which source it reads (s.source is "live" or "delayed") and can refuse delayed data with source: "live".

      Recordings on your computer

      While Strate follows an underlying (a chart shows its options levels, or a script reads it) and your connection is connected, the snapshots and the option trades are recorded on your computer. Replay and long backtests read these recordings.

      • To record an underlying every trading day, even when no chart shows its options, tick Record the chain even when the indicator is hidden in the Recordings window, Options tab (SPX is ticked by default; NDX, SPY and QQQ can be added). Strate then records it from 9:15 a.m. to the options close, New York time, while it is running and connected.
      • Recordings are kept for the number of sessions set in the Option GEX Heatmap settings (Sessions kept per underlying, 20 by default); older days answer not_kept. Chain recordings are read only while your options data connection is set up and connected, and changing the connection's keys erases them.
      • Recording started with the versions of October 31, 2026 (chain snapshots) and November 6, 2026 (option trades). Earlier days, and days the app did not follow, have no options data: scripts get available = false with a reason, never an error.
      • Option trades cover a followed subset of the chain, not the whole of it: today the 0DTE options of SPX and NDX in a band around the price, recentred as the price moves, and SPY and QQQ when a script or a display reads them.

      No look-ahead, by construction

      One rule applies in live, Replay, long backtests and the built-in check: a snapshot is visible at instant τ when its publication time, plus one second, is at or before τ. The script reads the latest visible one.

      • τ is the event's time in a handler, the close of a closed bar in the body, and the earlier of the close and "now" on the bar still forming (the clock live, the cursor in Replay).
      • The extra second covers the difference between your computer's clock and the exchange's timestamps. If your clock is measurably behind, the margin grows and warning W955 says so.
      • Option prints become visible one second after they are handed over; a multi-leg structure about 2.5 seconds after its first leg, once it is closed.
      • At equal times, order-by-order events, futures trades and book snapshots run first, then chain snapshots, then option events.

      So a bar recalculated later, or replayed, sees the same snapshots at the same times as it did live, within the small quantization of the recording. Exceptions: partial snapshots, provisional open interest, delayed snapshots and exact publication times are recorded only while a script reads options live; other periods replay with complete snapshots only, published at their assembly time. Days older than the sessions kept answer not_kept.

      What a script never gets

      • A script run from another computer, or while Strate is open to the network, receives no options data (account is "none").
      • A script never sees the settings of the app's options displays (expiry filters, manual offsets). Everything it uses is written in the script, so it gives the same result on every computer.

      A first script#

      Open the Indicators window of a chart, add StrateScript (preview) from the Custom category, and choose Empty script. In the Code mode, paste this and press Run (or ⌘↵):

      StrateScript
      indicator "Options status" { overlay: false }
s = options.chain()
print(s.available, s.reason, s.source, s.underlying)   // → true check synthetic SPX
plot(s.available ? s.age / 1s : na, title: "Greeks age (s)")

      options.chain() with no argument reads the index of the chart's family. The built-in check runs your script on a synthetic chain, which is why it prints true check synthetic SPX; on a live chart the same line prints true ok live SPX.

      The first script on option trades adds up call and put contracts as they print (a running total, since state keeps its value from bar to bar), leaving spread legs to option_block:

      StrateScript
      indicator "Option prints per bar" {
  overlay: false,
  option_trades: { underlyings: ["SPX"] }
}
state calls = 0
state puts = 0
on option_trade(t) {
  if t.spread_leg { return }                  // legs of spreads are counted in option_block
  if t.is_call { calls += t.sign * t.size } else { puts += t.sign * t.size }
}
st = option_trades.status()
plot(st.available ? calls : na, #26a69a, title: "Call contracts")
plot(st.available ? puts : na, #ef5350, title: "Put contracts")
bg_color(st.state == "gap" ? #80808033 : na)

      t.sign is +1 for a new print and −1 for a cancellation, so total += t.sign * f(t) stays exact for any f. Keep a script in My indicators to add it to any chart, or start from one of the built-in Options templates.

      The chain: options.chain()#

      Arguments

      Signature
      options.chain(underlying, source: "any", partial: false, basis:, ratio:)
      ArgumentValuesDefault
      underlying"SPX", "NDX", "SPY", "QQQ", written as a literal textThe index of the chart's family
      source:"any", "live" (your connection only), "delayed" (the delayed source only)"any"
      partial:true also shows a snapshot that is still loadingfalse: the latest complete snapshot
      basis:futures price minus index price, imposedmeasured
      ratio:futures price divided by ETF price, imposedmeasured

      options.chain() always returns a chain object, never na. The underlying must be a literal text (options.chain("SPY")), because Strate reads the script's text to decide which chains to load. options.underlyings lists the family's underlyings, index first.

      Always test available

      When no snapshot can be read, available is false and reason says why. Missing data is never an error. The codes are stable; reason_text gives the same reason as an English sentence.

      reasonWhen
      okA snapshot is visible.
      checkThe built-in check's synthetic chain is visible.
      no_options_for_instrumentThe chart is not ES, MES, NQ or MNQ, and no underlying was written.
      no_accountLive: no options data connection, and no delayed snapshot received.
      connection_downLive: the connection is set up but not delivering, and no delayed snapshot received.
      loadingLive: the first complete snapshot, or the expiries the script reads, are still loading.
      source_filteredOnly the other source is available (see source:).
      not_recordedNo snapshot is recorded for this day or this time.
      expiries_not_recordedReplay or long backtest: that time was recorded for other expiries only.
      before_first_snapshotThis day has snapshots, but none is published yet at this instant.
      not_keptThe day is older than the sessions you keep.
      StrateScript
      indicator "Why not" { overlay: false }
s = options.chain("NDX")
print(s.available, s.reason, s.conversion)   // → false not_recorded none
d = options.chain(source: "delayed")
print(d.available, d.reason)   // → false source_filtered
plot(s.available ? 1 : 0, title: "NDX available")

      On the check's ES chart there is no NDX chain, and no delayed one: hence the two printed reasons.

      Chain fields

      FieldMeaning
      available, reason, reason_text, underlyingWhether a snapshot is visible, why not, and which underlying.
      source, delay"live", "delayed", "synthetic" (the check) or "none"; 0s or 15m.
      time, greeks_time, price_timePublication time in Strate (visibility is decided by it only); time of the latest greeks; time of the underlying's price.
      age, staleThe script's instant minus greeks_time; true beyond 5 minutes (live) or 60 minutes (delayed). Information only.
      spot, chart_spotThe underlying's price, and the same price on the chart.
      conversion, basis, ratio, conversion_timeHow index prices become chart prices ("basis", "ratio", "manual", "none").
      partial, coverageA partial snapshot (with partial: true) and the share of gamma exposure it covers, 0 to 1.
      oi_provisional, oi_day, oi_previous_dayWhether the open interest is confirmed for the session, and which day it belongs to.
      closing, accountA session close from the delayed source; "connected" or "none" live, "unknown" elsewhere.
      count, band_low, band_highOptions in the snapshot; lowest and highest strike followed (index prices).

      Filters

      These named arguments select options in count, options, strikes, expiries, zero_gamma and gex_at. They combine with "and".

      FilterValues
      type:"call", "put", "both" (default)
      expiry:"all" (default), "0dte", "no0dte", "monthly"
      dte_min:, dte_max:days to expiry, fractions allowed
      expiry_day:a day or a list of days (days since 1970-01-01, like session.day)
      settlement:"am", "pm", "both" (default)
      strike_min:, strike_max:index (or ETF) prices
      price_min:, price_max:chart prices
      min_oi:minimum open interest

      "0dte" means the expiries of the reference day: after the 4 p.m. close and over the weekend it is the next session's expiry, never an empty list. "monthly" is the third Friday of the month (the Thursday before when that Friday is a market holiday).

      StrateScript
      indicator "Chain counts" { overlay: false }
s = options.chain()
print(s.count(), s.count(expiry: "0dte"), s.count(type: "call"), s.count(expiry: "monthly"))   // → 136 34 68 34
plot(s.count(expiry: "0dte", min_oi: 1000), title: "0DTE options with OI ≥ 1000")

      Chain methods

      MethodReturns
      s.options(filters…, max: 2000)A list of options (max from 1 to 20,000).
      s.count(filters…)The number of options kept (cheap).
      s.strikes(filters…, step:)Strike rows sorted by strike; step: groups strikes by a chart distance.
      s.option(strike, expiry_day, type, settlement:)One option, or na.
      s.expiries(filters…)Expiries: .day, .time, .settlement, .dte, .count.
      s.zero_gamma(filters…, range: 20, index: false)The zero-gamma level as a chart price (or an index price), or na.
      s.gex_at(index_price, filters…)Total dealer gamma exposure if the underlying were at that price.
      s.to_chart(index_price), s.to_index(chart_price)Conversion between index and chart prices.

      zero_gamma recomputes the gamma of every kept option with Black-Scholes along a range of prices, weighted by open interest (calls +, puts −), and returns the crossing nearest to the current price, searching within range percent and within the strikes followed. It is computed once per snapshot and set of filters, then cached.

      Option and strike fields

      Option fieldMeaning
      strike, priceStrike as an index price; the same strike as a chart price.
      is_callTrue for a call, false for a put (there is no type field).
      settlement, expiry, expiry_day, dte"am" or "pm"; expiry instant; expiry day; days to expiry.
      oi, gamma, ivOpen interest; gamma published by the source; implied volatility as a fraction.
      gex, dealer_gexgex: gamma exposure in dollars per 1 % move, always positive. dealer_gex: +gex for a call, −gex for a put (dealers assumed long calls, short puts, as on the app's charts).
      delta, feed_deltaBlack-Scholes delta recomputed by Strate (r = 0); delta published by the source. Choose your convention.
      prev_volume, prev_closePrevious session's volume and closing price of the option.
      vanna, charmDealer vanna and charm, the same method as the app's displays. Estimates from open interest, not dealers' actual positions.

      A strike row from s.strikes() has strike, price, call_oi, put_oi, call_gex, put_gex, net_gex, vanna, charm, options, call_prev_volume and put_prev_volume. The walls below use the app's definition (largest call and put gamma exposure); they are a recipe, so change it as you like.

      StrateScript
      indicator "Option walls" { overlay: true }
exp = input.choice("Expiries", ["all", "0dte", "no0dte", "monthly"], "all")
s = options.chain()
cw = na
pw = na
bestC = 0
bestP = 0
if s.available {
  for k in s.strikes(expiry: exp) {
    if k.call_gex > bestC { bestC = k.call_gex; cw = k.price }
    if k.put_gex > bestP { bestP = k.put_gex; pw = k.price }
  }
}
plot(cw, #26a69a, title: "Call wall", style: "step")
plot(pw, #ef5350, title: "Put wall", style: "step")
plot(s.available ? s.zero_gamma(expiry: exp) : na, #e8c15a, title: "Zero gamma", style: "dashed")

      Chart prices and index prices

      An index and its futures contract do not trade at the same price, and an ETF trades at a fraction of its index. Every price of the options world (strike, spot, strike_min…) is an index or ETF price; every field named price (and chart_spot, to_chart, zero_gamma) is a chart price.

      • Index (SPX on ES or MES, NDX on NQ or MNQ): chart price = index price + basis.
      • ETF (SPY, QQQ): chart price = ETF price × ratio.

      The engine measures the conversion on the chart's own trades: the last trade at or before the snapshot's price time, at most 60 seconds earlier, never a later one. Without such a trade, chart prices are na and warning W954 appears. basis: or ratio: impose the conversion.

      StrateScript
      indicator "Conversion" { overlay: true }
s = options.chain()
print(s.conversion, s.to_chart(s.spot) == s.chart_spot)   // → basis true
m = options.chain(basis: 20)
print(m.conversion, m.basis)   // → manual 20
plot(s.chart_spot, #e8c15a, title: "Index on the chart", style: "step")

      Which expiries are loaded

      Strate reads your script's text to decide which expiries to load. The script loads only the expiries it uses when every call that depends on expiries has a literal filter: expiry: "0dte", "no0dte" or "monthly", dte_max: N, or expiry_day: J. Anything else (a variable, a calculation, expiry: "all", no filter) loads every expiry, which takes longer.

      StrateScript
      indicator "0DTE gamma" { overlay: false }
s = options.chain()
plot(s.available ? s.count(expiry: "0dte") : na, title: "0DTE options")
plot(s.available ? s.zero_gamma(expiry: "0dte") : na, title: "0DTE zero gamma")

      New snapshots: on options

      Signature
      on options(s) { … }

      The handler runs once for each snapshot published, for each underlying the script reads; s.underlying tells which one. Declare it at the top level. Partial snapshots run it only when the script contains partial: true somewhere.

      StrateScript
      indicator "Call wall moves" { overlay: true }
state last = na
on options(s) {
  if s.available and s.underlying == "SPX" {
    best = 0
    w = na
    for k in s.strikes(type: "call") {
      if k.call_gex > best { best = k.call_gex; w = k.price }
    }
    if not na(last) and not na(w) and w != last { label.new(s.time, w, "call wall " + str(last) + " → " + str(w)) }
    last = w
  }
}
plot(close, title: "Close")

      A handler that only stores what it needs in state variables, and lets the body draw, is usually cheaper.

      Data quality

      Every snapshot says what it is; nothing is filtered behind your back.

      • Source. With the default source: "any", the chain follows what the app delivers and never flips back and forth between real-time and delayed. When it switches to delayed, greeks_time can move back by about 15 minutes.
      • Three times. time is when the snapshot became visible; greeks_time and price_time say how old its contents are. Greeks are refreshed in blocks, so age is often above a minute even in real time.
      • Partial snapshots appear in the first minutes after Strate starts following an underlying. They are ignored unless you pass partial: true.
      • Open interest is published once a day, early in the morning, New York time. Before that, the snapshot carries the previous figures: oi_provisional and oi_previous_day say so.
      StrateScript
      indicator "Options quality" { overlay: false }
s = options.chain(partial: true)
print(s.source, s.partial, s.coverage, s.oi_provisional, s.oi_previous_day, s.closing, s.account)   // → synthetic false 1 false false false unknown
plot(s.available and not s.stale ? 1 : 0, title: "Fresh")

      Market totals: options.totals

      options.totals(u) is ready for the source's own whole-market totals (volumes, put/call ratio, IV by expiry). Today your options data connection does not provide these totals: available is false and reason is "not_provided", so a script written for them runs and shows nothing rather than a zero.

      StrateScript
      indicator "Market put/call" { overlay: false }
m = options.totals("SPX")
plot(m.available ? m.put_call_ratio : na, title: "P/C (whole market)")

      Option trades#

      Besides the chain, scripts read the option prints themselves, one at a time, and the blocks Strate builds from them. Nothing is computed for you: totals, ratios and profiles are scripts, which keeps every definition in your hands.

      The header: option_trades:

      Settings live in the script's header. Every key is optional, and every key except underlyings accepts an input.* setting. The header alone loads nothing: a handler or a call to option_trades.… does.

      KeyDefaultRole
      underlyingsthe chart family's indexWhich feeds to load: a literal list among "SPX", "NDX", "SPY", "QQQ".
      fromrollingFirst instant at which the handlers run: session, rth, start, rolling, "HH:MM", auto, launch.
      bursts, burst_delay, burst_bytrue, 1000ms, "side"Group successive orders of one option into bursts.
      buy_from, sell_to1.0, 0.0Side of a simple order from its position in the bid-ask spread (default: strictly at the ask / at the bid).
      structure_wait, structure_tolerance, by_condition1500ms, 3ms, trueHow the legs of a structure are assembled.
      side_margin, edge_margin0.25, 25When a structure counts as bought or sold; when it may be incomplete.
      backfill"separate"Prints sent again at subscription: kept apart, merged, or dropped.
      retention15mMemory of prints, to match cancellations, corrections and duplicates (5 min to 4 h). Longer uses more memory.
      min_print_size, min_block_size1Skip small prints or blocks before the handler runs.
      max_bar_events, fuel_per_daysee errorsProtection limits (E957, E967).
      basis, ratiomeasuredConversion to chart prices imposed, as in options.chain.

      Prints: on option_trade(t)

      Runs for every print of the requested underlyings, legs of spreads included, and for cancellations and corrections:

      • a cancellation repeats every field of the original print; only sign changes (−1);
      • a correction gives two calls in a row: kind = "cancel" (old value) then kind = "new", both with correction = true;
      • a cancellation whose origin is unknown arrives with matched = false and every amount at 0, so a total is never poisoned (W959 counts them);
      • duplicates are dropped.
      FieldsMeaning
      time, trade_time, recv_time, session_day, id, seqVisibility time; exchange trade time; hand-over time; session day; rank of the print in the day; feed sequence.
      kind, correction, sign, matched"new" or "cancel"; part of a correction; +1 / −1; origin found.
      underlying, root, symbol, strike, is_call, expiry, expiry_day, dte, years, settlement, multiplierThe option. strike is an index price: use it as the key of a total by strike.
      price, chart_priceThe strike as a chart price. Not the premium.
      premium, size, notionalContract price, contracts, premium × size × multiplier.
      bid, ask, side, dir, position, feed_sideQuote carried by the print; "buy", "sell", "mid" or "unknown"; +1, −1, 0 or na; position in the spread; raw aggressor flag of the feed.
      oi, spot, iv, iv_sourceOpen interest known at the print; underlying price; implied volatility and where it came from.
      delta, gamma, vega, theta, vanna, charmBlack-Scholes greeks of the option at that spot and IV.
      delta_dollars, gamma_dollars, future_equivalentAmounts signed on the customer side by dir; 0 at mid.
      spread_leg, backfill, in_gap, exchange, conditionLeg of a structure; sent again at subscription; received during a gap; raw feed flags.

      Blocks: on option_block(b)

      Runs for each action of the grouping, when it becomes true: a block appears, a burst grows, a structure closes, a block is cancelled or corrected (b.kind: "appear", "grow", "cancel", "correct"). The legs of a structure never appear alone here. A block is an order or a burst of one option, or a multi-leg structure: verticals, ratios, straddles, strangles, risk reversals, butterflies, condors, iron condors and iron butterflies, calendars, diagonals…

      One rule for totals. b is the state after the action and b.prev the state delivered at the previous call for this block (na at appear). Context (spot, IV, greeks, chart price) is frozen in the block at its first piece, so adding value(b) and removing value(b.prev) is exact:

      StrateScript
      indicator "Customer delta of blocks" {
  overlay: false,
  option_trades: { underlyings: ["SPX"] }
}
state d = 0
on option_block(b) { d += b.delta_dollars - (na(b.prev) ? 0 : b.prev.delta_dollars) }
plot(d / 1e6, title: "Customer delta ($M)")

      Useful block fields: is_structure, family, name, short_name, size, buy_size, sell_size, notional, net_notional, delta_dollars, gamma_dollars, future_equivalent, strike, price, side ("bought", "sold", "undetermined"… for a structure), dir, net, mid, ratio, incomplete, alerts and legs (each leg with its own strike, side, premium, greeks and amounts).

      Feed events: on option_feed(e)

      e.kindWhen
      "gap_start"Start of a loss known at the time (connection down, full queue, sleep).
      "gap"At the known end of a gap, with start, end, cause and lost.
      "coverage"The list of followed options changed (band, expiries, added, removed).
      "open", "close"The feed or the recorder started or stopped.

      Status and coverage

      option_trades.status(u) and option_trades.coverage(u) read the feed at the script's instant, so a closed bar gives the same value live and in Replay.

      • status: state ("ok", "gap", "not_recorded", "before_first_event", "not_kept", "check"), available, reason_text, recorded, since, last, gaps, lost, unmatched, duplicates, live_state…
      • coverage: count, strike_low, strike_high, price_low, price_high, expiries, symbols, time, and two methods: covers(strike, expiry_day, "call"|"put") and covered_time(…, since: t).
      • option_trades.to_chart(strike, u) and option_trades.to_index(price, u) convert at the script's instant.
      A strike that is not followed is not a zero.

      Prints cover the followed band only. Test coverage() before reading "nothing traded" into an empty strike.

      Black-Scholes: bs.*#

      Signature
      bs.price(type, spot, strike, years, iv, rate: 0, dividend: 0)
bs.delta / bs.gamma / bs.vega / bs.theta / bs.vanna / bs.charm (same arguments)
bs.iv(type, premium, spot, strike, years, rate: 0, dividend: 0)

      Pure functions, with no data, available everywhere. type is "call" or "put", years the time to expiry in years, iv and rate as fractions (0.2 = 20 %). Units: price in points of the underlying; gamma per point; vega per unit of volatility (× 0.01 per volatility point); theta and charm per year (÷ 365 per calendar day). An invalid constant ("straddle", negative years, iv at or below 0) gives E954; an invalid computed value gives na. bs.* alone loads no options data.

      StrateScript
      indicator "Black-Scholes" { overlay: false }
print(bs.price("call", 100, 100, 1, 0.2, rate: 0.05))   // → 10.450584

      Absorption with zero gamma Simulated strategy

      Order-by-order absorption at the traded price, taken only on the chosen side of zero gamma, with the snapshot visible at that trade. Which side is "right" is your hypothesis: it is a setting.

      Needs: chain snapshots and order-by-order (MBO) data

      Show the code
      StrateScript
      strategy "Absorption with zero gamma" { overlay: true, mbo_from: rth, level_history: 2m, latency: 60ms, costs: { commission: 2.5 usd, slippage: 1 tick } }

win = input.duration("Window", 30s, group: "Absorption")
minRank = input.float("Min aggressor volume rank", 97, min: 0, max: 100, group: "Absorption")
normWin = input.duration("Normalize over", 30m, group: "Absorption")
heldPct = input.float("Held at best (% of window)", 60, min: 0, max: 100, group: "Absorption")
maxMove = input.int("Max price move (ticks)", 2, min: 0, group: "Absorption")
rule = input.choice("Take", ["longs above zero gamma, shorts below", "longs below zero gamma, shorts above"], "longs above zero gamma, shorts below", group: "Options")
exp = input.choice("Expiries", ["all", "0dte", "no0dte", "monthly"], "all", group: "Options")
src = input.choice("Options source", ["live", "any"], "live", group: "Options")
maxAge = input.duration("Max greeks age", 5m, group: "Options")
stopTicks = input.int("Stop (ticks)", 8, min: 1, group: "Exits")
targetTicks = input.int("Target (ticks)", 16, min: 1, group: "Exits")

on trade(t) {
  lv = mbo.level(t.price, window: win)
  // normalized at every trade, before any condition
  rk = norm.rank(na(lv) ? na : lv.aggressor_volume, normWin, min_count: 200)
  // the snapshot visible at this trade; zero gamma is computed once per snapshot
  g = options.chain(source: src)
  zg = g.available and g.age <= maxAge ? g.zero_gamma(expiry: exp) : na
  absorbed = not na(lv) and rk >= minRank and lv.time_at_best >= win * heldPct / 100 and lv.displayed > 0 and abs(t.price - open) / instrument.tick <= maxMove
  if absorbed and not na(zg) and position.flat and position.pending == 0 {
    above = t.price > zg
    longsAbove = rule == "longs above zero gamma, shorts below"
    // sellers hitting a bid that holds: buyers absorb; buyers lifting an offer that holds: sellers absorb
    if t.is_sell and (above == longsAbove) {
      enter("buy", stop: price(stopTicks), target: price(targetTicks), timeout: 20m, tag: "bid holds")
    } else if t.is_buy and (above != longsAbove) {
      enter("sell", stop: price(stopTicks), target: price(targetTicks), timeout: 20m, tag: "offer holds")
    }
  }
}

// so the legs of a spread are never counted twice. Options trades exist on recorded days only (since 2026-11-06).
// retention: 15m keeps memory low enough to add underlyings (a cancellation more than 15 min after its print then adds 0).
// Rule b / b.prev: add the block, remove what was added for its previous state (value() reads only fields of the block).
indicator "Net premium flow" {
  overlay: false,
  option_trades: { underlyings: ["SPX"], from: session, retention: 15m,
                   buy_from: input.float("Buy from (position in spread)", 1.0, min: 0.5, max: 1, group: "Side"),
                   sell_to: input.float("Sell up to (position in spread)", 0.0, min: 0, max: 0.5, group: "Side") }

state calls = 0
state puts = 0

fn keep(leg) {
  if moneyness == "all" or na(leg.spot) { return true }
  otm = leg.is_call ? leg.strike > leg.spot : leg.strike < leg.spot
  return moneyness == "otm" ? otm : not otm
}
fn amount(leg) {
  if unit == "delta $" { return nz(leg.delta_dollars) }   // already signed (customer side)

}

    if leg.is_call { calls += k * amount(leg) } else { puts += k * amount(leg) }
  }
}
on session(s) { if s.kind == "session_open" { calls = 0 ; puts = 0 } }
on option_block(b) {
  add(b, 1)
  add(b.prev, -1)
}

      Dealer Gamma Flow Built-in template

      Gamma, delta or futures equivalent dealers would have added today, assuming they take the other side of every customer trade (a convention the script makes explicit), SPX and SPY in one unit.

      Needs: option trades

      Show the code
      StrateScript
      // Template: dealer gamma flow, ASSUMING the dealer takes the other side of every customer trade (a convention, not a fact).
// Simple orders from option_trade (t.sign undoes cancellations), structures from option_block (b / b.prev rule): no double count.
// gamma_dollars = dollars of delta per 1 % move, signed on the customer side; the dealer is the opposite.
// Near expiry the gamma of a 0DTE option explodes (T -> 0): without a cutoff, the prints of the last minutes make most of the day's
// total. They are skipped by default (input below, 0 = keep all). The total also keeps what expired options added until the session
// resets: read it as flow added today, not as the dealers' position now.
indicator "Dealer gamma flow" {
  overlay: false,
  option_trades: { underlyings: ["SPX", "SPY"], from: session, backfill: "drop", retention: 15m }
}
measure = input.choice("Measure", ["gamma $", "delta $", "futures"], "gamma $")
skipLast = input.int("Skip the last minutes before expiry", 30, min: 0)
state flow = 0
fn val(x) { return measure == "gamma $" ? x.gamma_dollars : measure == "delta $" ? x.delta_dollars : x.future_equivalent }
// years = time to expiry used by the greeks, fixed at the print (the same for its cancellation)
fn keep(x) { return na(x.years) or x.years * 525600 >= skipLast }
fn legs(x) {
  if na(x) { return 0 }
  s = 0
  for leg in x.legs { if keep(leg) { s += nz(val(leg)) } }
  return s
}
on session(s) { if s.kind == "session_open" { flow = 0 } }
on option_trade(t) {
  if t.spread_leg or not keep(t) { return }        // structures go through option_block
  v = val(t)
  if na(v) { return }
  flow += -t.sign * v                              // dealer = opposite of the customer
}
on option_block(b) {
  // no test on b.dir: a leg whose side is unknown adds 0, so b / b.prev stays exact when a leg is cancelled
  if not b.is_structure { return }
  flow += -(legs(b) - legs(b.prev))
}
ok = option_trades.status("SPX").available
plot(ok ? flow : na, #2962ff, title: "Dealer flow added today")
plot(ok ? norm.zscore(flow, 500) : na, #e8c15a, title: "z-score")

      Limits, pitfalls and common errors#

      Pitfalls

      • Call or put: read o.is_call. An option has no type field; o.type is E101.
      • Not every bar has options. Test s.available (or na(x)) before using a value.
      • The underlying must be literal. options.chain(u) with a variable is E951.
      • Index price or chart price? strike and spot are index prices; price and chart_spot are chart prices. A strike plotted on an ES chart lands tens of points away.
      • t.price is not the premium. It is the strike as a chart price; the contract price is t.premium.
      • Key by strike, draw with to_chart. The conversion moves during the day.
      • Double counting. Prints include spread legs: filter t.spread_leg, or work in on option_block.
      • Always multiply by t.sign, and in blocks add value(b) and remove value(b.prev). Never skip a block because b.dir is na.
      • 0DTE greeks near expiry. Greeks are fixed at the print and the gamma of a 0DTE option explodes in its last minutes: skip prints with little time left (t.years), as Dealer Gamma Flow does.
      • Delayed data looks current. Its time is recent while its content is 15 minutes old: a strategy should pass source: "live" or test s.source and s.age.
      • "0dte" after the close is the next session, never an empty list.
      • One unfiltered call loads every expiry. Filter every call with a literal to load less.
      • One budget per bar. The first zero_gamma on a new snapshot is the expensive call; keep the number of different filter sets small.
      • on options runs for each underlying the script reads: test s.underlying.
      • 500 drawn objects at most. Labels beyond 500 disappear, oldest first: raise the threshold.
      • The last second of a bar belongs to the next bar, because of the one-second visibility margin.

      CodeCause

      E952Invalid argument or filter (expiry: "weekly", dte_min above dte_max…).
      E953max:, range: or step: out of bounds.

      What is not available

      • Trading options. Strategies are simulations on ES, MES, NQ and MNQ.
      • An options order book or continuous option quotes: you get chain snapshots and prints.
      • Options on futures, and underlyings other than SPX, NDX, SPY and QQQ.
      • Whole-market totals today (options.totals answers "not_provided").
      • Options data for days before recording started, or days the app did not follow.
      • Automated execution of strategies is not part of the current version.

      Get started#

      1. Download Strate and open a chart of ES, MES, NQ or MNQ.
      2. Connect your options data account in Strate's connections. It is your own account, read only. Without it, the app can still show the delayed public source on its charts.
      3. Open StrateScript: Indicators window, Custom category, StrateScript (preview). Load an Options template from the script menu, or paste a script from this page.
      4. Or ask Strate AI, in the panel's Assistant mode, to write or explain a script. It is optional: every script here works without it.
      5. Replay a recorded day to see your script on past sessions, or run a long backtest from the strategy Report over a folder of local MBO replay files, one per day (see Live, Replay, long backtest and the check).

      Want to see Strate first? Try the interactive demo.
