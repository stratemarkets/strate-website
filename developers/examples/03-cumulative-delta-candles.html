      Cumulative delta as candles

      The cumulative delta of the session drawn as candles in its own pane: each candle opens at the previous close of the cumulative delta and moves by the bar's delta, with the bar's intrabar delta extremes as wicks when they are known.

      StrateScript
      indicator "CVD candles" { overlay: false, format: "compact", labels: true }

reset = input.choice("Reset", ["session", "rth", "day", "none"], "session")

cvd = of.cvd(reset: reset)
prevCvd = cvd - nz(delta)
hi = na(bar.delta_high) ? max(prevCvd, cvd) : prevCvd + bar.delta_high
lo = na(bar.delta_low) ? min(prevCvd, cvd) : prevCvd + bar.delta_low

candle(prevCvd, hi, lo, cvd, cvd >= prevCvd ? #26a69a : #ef5350)
hline(0, color: #888888, style: "dotted")

      What to notice:

      • of.cvd(reset:) resets at the chosen boundary; prevCvd is the value before this bar's delta;
      • bar.delta_high / bar.delta_low are the running-delta extremes inside the bar, or na when the source does not provide them, hence the fallback.
